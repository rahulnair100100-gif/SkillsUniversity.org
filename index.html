SkillsUniversity serves as an online training academy and coaching platform, where entrepreneurs and aspiring entrepreneurs learn how to use AI tools to grow their business — and where children aged 10 to 16 learn how AI works and how to use it safely.

Until now, SkillsUniversity.org has trained 3,000+ entrepreneurs and 100+ businesses on how to grow their business.
